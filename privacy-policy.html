Privacy Policy

Easy Group Join    Last Updated: June 21, 2026

Easy Group Join ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using Easy Group Join, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Anonymous Device Identifier

We generate a unique anonymous identifier for your device the first time you use the app. This identifier is stored locally on your device and is used to track your submitted groups, liked groups, and reported groups.

It is not linked to your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

b) Location Information

With your permission, the app detects your approximate country and city using your device's location services. This is used only to indicate the general origin of group submissions.

We do not collect your precise GPS coordinates. You may deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

c) Group Submission Information

  • Invite link URL
  • Group or channel name
  • Description
  • Category and platform type
  • Country and city
  • Submission timestamp
  • Group image uploaded or auto-fetched

d) Usage Statistics

  • Number of group profile views
  • Number of join link clicks
  • Number of likes received
  • Number of user reports received

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display approved group and channel submissions to users
  • To prevent duplicate group submissions
  • To enforce community guidelines and moderate content
  • To show your submissions in the My Groups section
  • To maintain usage statistics such as views, clicks, likes, and reports
  • To detect misuse or policy violations
  • To improve the quality and safety of the directory

3. Data Storage

Your submitted data is stored in a secure Google Sheets database managed by our team.

Approved group submissions are publicly visible within the app. Pending and rejected submissions are accessible only to our content review team.

We take reasonable technical and organizational precautions to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Advertising - Google AdMob

Easy Group Join is a free app supported by advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC.

Google AdMob may collect limited device identifiers, advertising IDs, and usage data to serve relevant ads according to Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob policies.

Where ads may appear in the app:

  • Banner ads on Home, Category, My Groups, Add Group, and Group Detail screens
  • Interstitial ads before opening WhatsApp, Telegram, or Messenger group links
  • Rewarded ads when submitting a group or channel
  • No ads appear on the About screen

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sheets for data storage and content management
  • Google AdMob for displaying ads
  • Google Play Services for app distribution and device integration
  • Expo / React Native for app functionality
  • Device location services for country and city detection

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or marketing companies beyond what is described in this policy.

6. Data Retention

Group submissions are retained for as long as the listing remains active in the app.

Rejected or removed groups may be permanently deleted from our records at any time.

The anonymous device identifier stored on your device is cleared when you uninstall the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Easy Group Join is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has submitted information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personally identifiable information such as names or email addresses, we are unable to identify specific user records by individual identity.

If you want a specific group submission removed, contact us with the group details and we will review your request.
